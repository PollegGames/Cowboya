## Energy and Health Bots

- **EnergyBot owns energy**: stores per-action costs (walk, jump, crouch, attack, grab), updates `RobotStats`, raises `OnEnergyChanged`, and drives faint state when depleted. Uses `TryConsume(EnergyAction, deltaTime, overrideCost)` / `HasEnergyForAction` for checks. Robots auto-recharge up to `MaxEnergy` using `rechargeRate`/`tickDelay`; leave `autoRecharge` enabled on the player to allow the same recharge loop (player flag no longer disables it).
- **Action wiring**: `RobotLocomotionController` spends walking/jumping/crouching energy through `EnergyBot`; attack flows (`PerformAttackByEnergy`, `AttackHandler`, player attack request) call `TryConsume` for attack costs; `CowboyGrabController` spends grab energy before attaching items.
- **State controller**: `RobotStateController` delegates energy updates to `EnergyBot` and uses health events to change state to `Dead`. Energy depletion from `EnergyBot` transitions to `Faint`; recovering enough energy restores `Alive`.
- **HealthBot role**: emits `OnHealthChanged` and routes damage to `RobotBrain` for enemies or `PlayerBrain` for the player. Damage feedback stays handled here.
- **Player brain**: new `PlayerBrain` centralizes player-only logic (faint on energy zero, dead on health zero, and energy spends for attacks/grabs). It flags the attached `EnergyBot` as player-controlled to prevent automatic recharge and keeps movement/attack/grab systems coordinated through the same brain.
- **Setup checklist**: every robot should include `EnergyBot` + `HealthBot` + `RobotStateController`. Configure per-action energy on `EnergyBot` (defaults can be overridden by locomotion costs). Ensure the player prefab also has `PlayerBrain`; keep `EnergyBot`/`HealthBot` references assigned or discoverable on the same GameObject.
